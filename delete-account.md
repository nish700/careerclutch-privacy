# Delete Your CareerClutch Account

Effective Date: May 9, 2026 | Last Updated: May 9, 2026

You have full control over your CareerClutch account and data. This page explains how to request account deletion and what data is removed.

## How to Request Account Deletion

To delete your CareerClutch account and all associated cloud data, send a request to:

📧 **[email protected]**

**Please include in your email:**

- The email address associated with your CareerClutch account
- Subject line: **"Account Deletion Request"**
- Optionally: the reason for deletion (helps us improve)

**What happens next:**

1. We will verify your identity by replying to the email registered with your account
2. Once verified, we will process your deletion request within **7 business days**
3. You will receive a confirmation email once your account and data have been deleted

## Clearing Local Data on Your Device

If you want to remove only the data stored on your device (without deleting your cloud account), you can do this directly from within the app at any time:

1. Open the CareerClutch app
2. Go to **Profile → Clear All Data**
3. Confirm when prompted

This clears local cache, interview history stored on the device, and downloaded resume drafts. Your cloud account remains active and your data on our servers is preserved. To fully delete your account, please email us as described above.

## What Data Is Deleted on Account Deletion

When we process your deletion request, the following data is permanently removed from our systems:

- **Account information**: name, email address, profile details
- **Authentication credentials**: passwords, OAuth tokens
- **Interview history**: all mock interview sessions, transcripts, scores, and feedback
- **Resume content**: any resumes you uploaded, parsed data, optimized drafts
- **Practice data**: streaks, progress metrics, weakness analysis, skill development history
- **Preferences**: notification settings, role preferences, target tracks
- **Subscription records**: account-linked billing history (we retain anonymized financial records as required by law)
- **Device tokens**: push notification identifiers

## What Data May Be Retained

Some data may be retained for limited periods even after account deletion, as required by law or for legitimate business purposes:

- **Anonymized analytics**: aggregated usage data with no personally identifiable information
- **Legal and financial records**: invoice and tax records as required by Indian tax law (typically 7 years)
- **Fraud prevention records**: limited identifiers if the account was flagged for abuse

This retained data cannot be linked back to you personally.

## Active Subscriptions

If you have an active subscription when you request account deletion:

- Your subscription will not be auto-renewed
- You will retain access until the end of your current billing period (per Google Play policy)
- For refunds on in-app purchases, please contact Google Play Support directly — refunds are handled by Google, not by CareerClutch

You can also manage or cancel your subscription independently through your Google Play account → Subscriptions.

## Reactivating an Account

Once your account is deleted, it cannot be recovered. If you wish to use CareerClutch again, you will need to create a new account. Previous interview history, resumes, and progress data cannot be restored.
